# Research Framework

Use this checklist before any trade decision.

## Evidence Minimum

Collect at least one source from each class:

1. Financial reporting/news
2. Company primary source (filing, earnings deck/call notes, press release)
3. Market commentary (analyst/blog)
4. Social sentiment signal

## Source Quality Rules

1. Prefer primary documents and reputable reporting over reposts.
2. Record publication date for every source.
3. Prefer information from the last 7 days for tactical decisions.
4. Cross-check major claims across at least two independent sources.
5. Flag and discount unverified social claims.

## Trade Scoring Rubric

Score each symbol from 1 (worst) to 5 (best):

1. Catalyst strength
2. Valuation/risk-reward
3. Balance-sheet resilience
4. Trend and momentum quality
5. Confidence in evidence

Compute a weighted score:

- 30% catalyst strength
- 20% valuation/risk-reward
- 15% balance-sheet resilience
- 15% trend and momentum
- 20% confidence in evidence

## Decision Rules

1. Trade only when weighted score >= 3.5 and evidence confidence >= 3.0.
2. Prefer no-trade when sources materially conflict.
3. Size down when macro risk is elevated or confidence is marginal.
4. Re-check live prices immediately before submitting trade commands.
